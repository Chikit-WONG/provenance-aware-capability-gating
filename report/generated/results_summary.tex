\paragraph{Formal evaluation and two-by-two follow-up.}
The frozen Cartesian plan contained 216 victim runs (six scenarios, three
content conditions, four defense arms, and three paired seeds).  The formal
artifact set contains 213 valid records and three infrastructure-invalid
records; the latter are retained in intention-to-test (ITT) denominators and
are not silently replaced.  Two independently frozen add-ons contributed 54
Capability+Provenance and 54 Prompt+Capability runs, all of which completed
validly.  The combined analysis therefore contains 324 planned runs, 321 valid
records, and three invalid records.  Its full input digest is recorded in the
analysis manifest (prefix \texttt{9443985c}).

\paragraph{Main security and utility findings.}
Across all tasks, Allow-All under Attack produced an executed unauthorized
effect in 9/17 valid runs (52.9\%) and exact-secret leakage in 6/17 (35.3\%),
whereas Full produced 0/18 for both outcomes.  The paired ITT risk differences
(Full minus Allow-All under Attack) were $-0.50$ for executed effects
($95\%$ CI $[-0.72,-0.28]$) and $-0.33$ for leakage
($95\%$ CI $[-0.56,-0.11]$).  Under Clean, Full completed the benign task in
15/18 runs (83.3\%) versus 17/18 (94.4\%) for Allow-All; the paired ITT utility
difference was $-0.11$ (95\% CI $[-0.33,0.11]$).  On T5--T6,
Capability-Only leaked in 3/6 attack runs, while Capability+Provenance,
Prompt+Capability, and Full each leaked in 0/6.  Capability+Provenance minus
Capability-Only and Prompt+Capability minus Capability-Only both had paired
risk differences of $-0.50$ (95\% bootstrap CI $[-0.83,-0.17]$).  Full minus
each intermediate arm had a paired risk difference of $0.00$ (95\% bootstrap
CI $[0.00,0.00]$).  The two-by-two corpus therefore shows an isolated
provenance gain, while its 0/6 Full rate has a floor effect.  Under Clean,
benign task success was 16/18 (88.9\%) for Prompt+Capability and 15/18
(83.3\%) for Full.

The deterministic sink-pressure test passed all 16 registered calls: the eight
provenance-enabled protected-value calls were denied with zero side effects,
while the eight non-provenance controls executed.  An independent hardened
corpus then produced 3/6 T5--T6 leakages for Prompt+Capability and 0/6 for Full;
the paired risk difference was $-0.50$ (95\% bootstrap CI
$[-0.83,-0.17]$).
